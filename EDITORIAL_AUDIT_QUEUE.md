# The Verifier Editorial Audit Queue

Version 1.1 · August 22, 2026

This file is a public editorial quality-control queue. It does not silently rewrite historical opinion pieces. Its purpose is to identify articles that need deliberate evidence, framing, freshness, or human-dignity review under `EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`.

## Review method

For each queued item, review:

1. **Classification** — confirm that opinion, analysis, reporting, advocacy, and unresolved allegations are clearly distinguished.
2. **Evidence** — add primary records or credible reporting for consequential factual claims.
3. **Freshness** — verify claims that depend on current officeholders, polling, war status, recognition counts, laws, alliances, or diplomatic positions.
4. **Attribution** — distinguish the author's conclusion from claims made by critics, governments, advocacy organizations, witnesses, or other interested parties.
5. **Human dignity** — criticize policies, ideologies, governments, organizations, or documented conduct without collectively condemning or dehumanizing populations.
6. **Corrections** — where a material factual statement is wrong or outdated, add a dated correction rather than silently overwriting the historical record.

## Priority C — repository-wide editorial standardization

The remaining files in `columns/` should receive a slower evidence pass over time, prioritizing:

- claims of crimes, corruption, fraud, conspiracy, or intentional misconduct;
- medical or scientific claims;
- election and polling claims;
- current officeholders or current law/policy;
- casualty figures, war-crime allegations, and conflict chronology;
- financial figures and market claims;
- allegations about named people or organizations;
- broad claims about demographic, ethnic, national, religious, political, or ideological groups.

The shared `assets/editorial-integrity.js` notice provides immediate classification and reader guidance while this evidence review proceeds. It is not a substitute for article-level sourcing and corrections.

## Status convention

Use these labels when items are reviewed:

- `QUEUED` — identified for review.
- `EVIDENCE ADDED` — consequential claims now include appropriate evidence.
- `FRAMING REVISED` — opinion/analysis/attribution distinctions improved.
- `CORRECTION ISSUED` — a dated material correction was added.
- `REVIEWED — NO MATERIAL CHANGE` — examined under the standard and retained as written.

Do not mark an item complete solely because it carries the global Editorial / Opinion banner.
